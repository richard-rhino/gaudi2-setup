# Qwen4Exp (Qwen3.8-Flash-Next) HPU port: validation on a tiny random-weight model

Model: `ports/qwen4_exp/tests/make_tiny.py` builds a 144.8M-parameter Qwen4Exp from the real `config.json` with the same
architecture features: 4 layers (3 Gated DeltaNet + 1 QSA), PLE at layer 2, hyper-connections (4 streams), 16 experts
top-4 + shared expert, real 248k vocab and tokenizer, head_dim 256 / partial RoPE 0.25 / interleaved MRoPE,
indexer_budget 2048. Reference: HF transformers `modeling_qwen4_exp` on CPU (bf16).
Prompts: 4 random token-id prompts of 7, 33, 130 and 300 tokens, run as one batch.

Metric: |logprob difference| between vLLM (HPU) and HF (CPU) for the same tokens. Top-1 agreement is also shown, but
a random-weight model has an almost flat 248k-way distribution, so top-1 flips on ~0.01 logprob noise; the logprob
difference is the meaningful number. bf16 noise level is ~0.002-0.003.

## Prefill (teacher-forced, every prompt position)
| Config | 7 tok | 33 tok | 130 tok | 300 tok |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TP=1 eager, tiny budget 64 (HF sparse beyond 64 tok, port dense) | 0.003 | 0.003 | 0.011 (max 0.110) | 0.026 (max 0.138) |
| TP=1 eager, budget 2048 (both dense) | 0.003 | 0.003 | 0.002 (max 0.012) | 0.002 (max 0.018) |
| TP=2 eager, budget 2048 | 0.003 | 0.003 | 0.003 (max 0.012) | 0.002 (max 0.014) |
Mean |dlogprob|. Top-1 agreement (budget 2048, TP=1): 6/6, 29/32, 125/129, 285/299.
The first row isolates the cost of running QSA dense instead of sparse (at budget 64 = 16 blocks of 4).

## Decode (32 greedy steps generated by vLLM, scored by HF on the same sequence)
| Config | 7 tok | 33 tok | 130 tok | 300 tok |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TP=1 eager | 0.003 (max 0.008) | 0.002 (0.008) | 0.003 (0.008) | 0.002 (0.004) |
| TP=1 eager, PLE history packed as bf16 | 0.003 (0.008) | 0.002 (0.008) | 0.003 (0.008) | 0.002 (0.004) |
| TP=2 eager | 0.002 (0.008) | 0.003 (0.008) | 0.002 (0.012) | 0.002 (0.008) |
| TP=1 torch.compile + HPU graphs (`enforce_eager=False`) | 0.002 (0.004) | 0.002 (0.008) | 0.003 (0.008) | 0.001 (0.004) |
Mean |dlogprob| (max). No drift across 32 steps (last-8-step mean 0.001-0.003), i.e. the GDN state, the PLE conv
history and the PLE token-id context carry across decode steps correctly.

Not validated (blocked by the driver crash, see FINDINGS 16): TP=4/8 on the tiny model (TP=4 needs a GDN state slot the
tiny model does not have; the real model fits up to TP=16), lazy mode, and the real 360 GB checkpoint.

## HF reference on HPU (Track A, `tests/hf_ref_check.py`)
The unmodified HF model runs on one HPU in eager mode after 4 workarounds (`tests/hpu_compat.py`): prefill logits vs
CPU argmax agreement 100%, rel. error 1.5% (bf16), 16/16 greedy tokens identical.
